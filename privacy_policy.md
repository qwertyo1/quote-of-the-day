## 2. Information We Store (and Don't Store)

### Personal Data
**We do not collect, process, or store any personal data.** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) to use the App.

### Local Device Settings & Cache
To provide a smooth experience, the App saves a few basic preferences **locally on your device** in private application storage:
- **Notification Settings**: Your preferred daily notification time and enabled status.
- **Language Preference**: Your selected quote language (System Default, English, or Russian).
- **Cached Quotes**: A local copy of quotes (with a 24-hour cache lifespan) to optimize performance, save mobile data, and enable offline reading.

**Data Retention & Lifetime:** All stored settings remain exclusively inside your phone's private storage until you clear app data or uninstall the App. Operating system level cloud backups (such as Android Auto Backup) are managed entirely by your Android system settings and personal Google account.

## 3. Network Operations & Third-Party Infrastructure

### Quote Content Downloads
The App communicates over encrypted HTTPS solely to download public, read-only quote files hosted on GitHub Content Delivery Network (`raw.githubusercontent.com`).

- **Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)):** Processing relies on Legitimate Interest to deliver requested quote content to your device.
- **Technical Request Headers:** Standard HTTP connections transmit your public IP address and network headers (User-Agent) to GitHub's hosting infrastructure. This technical connection data is processed transiently by GitHub Inc. (USA) per [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). We do not collect, log, or store IP addresses.
- **International Transfers:** Data transmission to GitHub servers in the United States relies on standard contractual clauses (SCCs) and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

### Offline Operation
If your device is offline or disconnected from the internet, the App operates entirely using your local quote cache without making outbound network requests.

## 4. Android Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

The App requests only essential Android permissions:

- 🌐 **Internet Access** (`android.permission.INTERNET`): Used solely to download public quote files over HTTPS.
- 🔔 **Notifications** (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Required on Android 13+ to display your scheduled daily quote notification.
- 🔄 **Reboot Schedule** (`android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): Used to restore your local daily notification schedule when your device restarts.

## 6. Your Choices, Controls & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data and permissions:

- **Adjust Notification Settings**: Open the App → **Settings** → Change notification time or toggle notifications.
- **Manage Device Permissions**: Open your phone's **Settings** → **Apps** → **Quote of the Day** → **Permissions** to enable or revoke permissions at any time.
- **Delete All Local Data**: Navigate to your phone's **Settings** → **Apps** → **Quote of the Day** → **Storage & Cache** → **Clear Storage** to instantly erase all local preferences and cached quotes.
- **Uninstalling the App**: Uninstalling the App immediately and permanently removes all local settings, cached quotes, and scheduled notifications from your device.

If you reside in the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), you hold statutory rights under the GDPR:
